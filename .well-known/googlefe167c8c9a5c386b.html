FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers before you book your ride.

Where is Jesse Private Chauffeur located?

We are located at Mariakani Road / Market Road Junction, Nanyuki, Kenya. You can use the Google Maps directions button on our website to find us.

How do I book a ride?

Fill in the booking form on the website, or contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp on 0790893508.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Airport pickups and drop-offs can be pre-booked so your chauffeur is ready for your journey.

Do you provide long-distance and road-trip services?

Yes. We provide chauffeur-driven long-distance trips and road trips across Kenya.

What vehicle is available?

Our featured vehicle is a Toyota Noah, 7-seater, automatic and hybrid, registration KDY 487X. Additional fleet vehicles can be arranged depending on availability.

Are you available 24/7?

Yes. Jesse Private Chauffeur is available 24/7. For late-night or early-morning trips, advance booking is recommended.

Can customers coming from outside Kenya book in advance?

Yes. Visitors and customers abroad can contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email and arrange their transport before arriving in Kenya.

How can I contact Jesse Private Chauffeur?

Call or WhatsApp 0790893508, or email jeyckim47@gmail.com.
